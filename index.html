Together

Explore with friends who have the same game version: everyone in the same room sees each other, on foot or flying as a visitor. Only your name, look and place are sent, through a public server anyone who knows the room can read.

    Game version

    Opens another version where you are. Ctrl and its number choose it too; drag the handles to change the order.

    Switching back to a version kept loaded is at once. Each one kept uses about as much memory as the one shown.

      Travel form

      Controls

      Flying

      Click look around · Esc let go of the mouse
      WASD fly
      Space / C up / down
      Shift fast · Wheel zoom
      Y / ShiftY faster / slower all the time
      G fly through walls · J cinematic camera

      Walking

      ` drop to the ground from the camera and walk, or fly again
      W / S run / backpedal · A / D turn
      Q / E strafe · Space jump
      Drag with the left button to turn the camera; hold the right button to turn yourself too (then A / D strafe); both buttons run
      Wheel zoom · \ or numpad / walk / run
      In water: Space / X up / down; Space at the surface jumps out
      1 … 0 emotes (set in the Emotes menu) · with Shift, over and over

      Going places

      / go to a zone, town, dungeon or raid
      Click the minimap to fly there
      O whole world · 1 / 2 continents (when flying)
      R back to the start
      Click the coordinates to copy a link to the view

      The world

      T / Shift+T later / earlier · N local time
      L torch · V grass and flowers
      B colour grading · X shadows (when flying) · Z fog
      F name colours for Alliance or Horde
      I dungeon and raid names · M music and sound
      H highlight chests, herbs, ore, fishing pools or anything by name
      Tab world map, filled in where you've explored · Esc off a flight

      Screen

      Click creatures and objects for their details
      P screenshot, everything in view in full detail
      Ctrl + 1 to 9, then 0: switch game version, in the Game version menu's order
      U hide the interface · K performance stats · F9 save a debug log
      ? or F1 this list

      Everything here is also in the menus along the top.
